## Interview-Specific Requirements

- Use synthetic candidate profiles and rubric notes only.
- Do not capture real candidate identifiers in scored competency examples.
- If a participant references a real candidate scenario, summarize behavior without naming people or organizations.
- Restrict interview session recordings to contributors supporting scoring/finalization UX remediation.
- Ensure `ux.interview.*` payloads exclude candidate names, free text, and sensitive notes.
