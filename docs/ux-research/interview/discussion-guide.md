# Moderated Interview Discussion Guide (30 Minutes)

## Goal
Evaluate how effectively interviewers can:
1. Start an interview session.
2. Score two competencies.
3. Finalize a draft summary.

## Session Setup
- **Format:** 1:1 moderated remote session.
- **Duration:** 30 minutes.
- **Participant profile:** New and intermediate interviewers.
- **Moderator tools:** Timer, task script, note template, consent script.

## Timeline and Script

### 0:00-3:00 — Welcome, Consent, and Context
- Thank participant and confirm recording/notes consent.
- Explain this is usability research on workflow and clarity, not participant performance.
- Confirm no real candidate data will be entered.

### 3:00-5:00 — Warm-up
- Ask: "Tell me about your interview experience and how you currently track notes or scores."
- Ask: "What part of interview documentation usually takes the most effort?"

### 5:00-22:00 — Core Workflow Tasks
Run tasks in order with think-aloud protocol.
- Prompt participant to narrate intent and expectations before each click.
- Only provide neutral prompts ("What would you do next?") when blocked.
- Record completion status, time-on-task, errors, and confidence score (1-5) per task.

### 22:00-27:00 — Reflection and Prioritization
- Ask participant to identify:
  - Most confusing screen or step.
  - Most useful assistive feature.
  - One change that would increase confidence in scoring accuracy.
- Ask confidence question: "How confident are you that your finalized draft is accurate and complete?"

### 27:00-30:00 — Close
- Confirm any final feedback.
- Reiterate privacy handling and anonymization.
- Thank participant and end session.

## Moderator Prompt Bank
Use these prompts to avoid leading participants:
- "What are you expecting to happen here?"
- "What tells you this action succeeded?"
- "What information is missing for your decision?"
- "If this were a real interview, what would you do now?"

## Scoring Focus Areas
For every session, capture observations tied to:
- **Efficiency:** Path length, hesitation, retries.
- **Effectiveness:** Task success and critical errors.
- **Confidence:** Self-reported confidence post-task and post-session.
